This is an experimental alpha. The 0.1.2 alpha adds country and state browsing, with a forest green, warm cream and muted orange palette. The improved audio buffers are retained. The new region browser still needs a physical FM-1 test. Back up your projects with your current firmware’s tools before installing.
01 / WANDER

Let the world choose.

Worldwide random on HOME, or press SAVE to browse 162 countries and 1,560 states and provinces. OCT− and OCT+ stay within your selected region.

02 / FIND YOUR BEARINGS

A globe beside the sound.

A static globe shows the recording’s real coordinates in forest green, warm cream and muted orange. Location and title sit on the left; press SEL for author and licence details.

03 / CONNECT

WiFi lives on GLO.

Scan for a 2.4 GHz network, enter its password on the keys, and listen. PLAY starts or stops; MASTER sets the volume.

A small departure

Plug in. Set up. Listen.

No new hardware is needed. The installer also includes stock-firmware restore and crash-report tools.

Source: MPL-2.0, with the original platform and third-party notices retained. Independent of Radio Aporee and M-VAVE.

  1. Connect your FM-1 directly by a USB data cable. Open the installer in desktop Chrome or Edge and allow MIDI access.
  2. Install the alpha. Keep the cable connected until the installer finishes.
  3. Join WiFi on GLO. The first location-index download takes about three minutes or longer on a weak signal.
  4. Listen to a random location. Press SAVE for country → state → Play random here. HOME returns to worldwide random. Choosing a new region needs another index scan; then skips use its cached locations.
